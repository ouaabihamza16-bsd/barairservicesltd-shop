What we do

Compressed air, from installation to breakdown

One team for your whole system: compressors, air treatment and pipework, whatever the make or model.

01

Compressor installation

Supply and installation of compressors as part of a complete package, including bespoke systems built into external containers to save space and reduce noise.

02

Pipework installation

A full range of pipework to suit your application and pressure: aluminium, galvanised, carbon and stainless steel, nylon and copper, with crimped, push-fit or threaded fittings. We are authorised installers of Prevost pipework systems.

03

Servicing & repairs

We repair, service and upgrade all makes and models of screw, vane and piston compressors, both oil-flooded and oil-free, including Mattei, ABAC, Bambi, HPC, Ingersoll Rand and Renner.

04

Air treatment & ancillaries

We supply, install, service and repair desiccant, membrane and refrigerant dryers, inline filters, automatic drains for receivers and compressors, and oil/water separators for condensate disposal.

05

Air conditioning & refrigeration

Air conditioning and refrigeration services alongside our compressed air work. Call us to talk through your requirements.

06

24/7 breakdown service

Our engineers are here to help keep you up and running at all times, day or night.

About us

A family-run compressed air business in Somerset

B.A.R Air Services Ltd is a family-run business based in Chilcompton, near Radstock, specialising in compressed air. Our installation and service teams look after compressors, air treatment and pipework, from the first site survey to ongoing servicing and breakdown support.

We are distributors for Mattei, Bambi, Abac and Prevost Pipework, providing national coverage across the UK, and authorised installers of Prevost pipework systems.
